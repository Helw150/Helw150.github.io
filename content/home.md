I work at Open Athena training open-source LLMs as part of the [Marin Project](https://marin.community/). My research has focused on making language technology more robust and adaptable to new modalities, languages, and domains. Much of my work focuses on data-centered machine learning approaches in service of that goal.

I received my PhD from Georgia Tech, advised by [Diyi Yang](https://cs.stanford.edu/~diyiy/), and spent part of that time as a visiting student at Stanford. During my PhD, I interned at Meta on Llama pretraining and at Google on Assistant.
