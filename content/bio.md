<p>I am a Member of Technical Staff at Open Athena, working on <a href="https://marin.community/">Marin</a>. Previously, I was a PhD student in Machine Learning at Georgia Tech, advised by Prof. <a href="https://cs.stanford.edu/~diyiy/">Diyi Yang</a>, and a visiting researcher at the Stanford NLP Group. During my PhD, I interned at Google AI working on Google Assistant and at Meta GenAI on pretraining for Llama 2, 3, and 4.</p>
          <p>My research focuses on methods for rapidly adapting language technology to new domains and modalities. The goal is to make fundamental language technologies more robust and easier to adapt, so that they can be a foundation for innovation by a broader population. During my PhD, I worked on the following topics:</p>

          <p>Developing methods to quickly adapt language technology to new &amp; diverse settings with little to no additional training data.
